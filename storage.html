How the totals work

Under the proposed stacking rules:

  • Lifetime Eternity+: 10 MB included
  • Monthly Eternity+: 2 MB while subscribed
  • Lifetime +2 MB pack: 12 MB total
  • Lifetime +7 MB pack: 17 MB total
  • Lifetime +16 MB pack: 26 MB total
  • Lifetime with all three packs: 35 MB total

These totals describe the proposed plan only. Purchased add-on capacity and subscription-included capacity must be tracked separately by the actual cloud service.
