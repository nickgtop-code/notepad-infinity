Help center

Questions? Fair enough.

Here is what the project plans to offer, and what is not available yet.

The premium checkout and cloud service are not live yet. No purchase should be treated as complete until an official checkout is connected and confirmed.
Is Notepad Infinity free?

The plan is to keep the core offline app free forever. Eternity+ is intended to offer optional premium features.

Can I buy Eternity+ right now?

Not through this version of the site. The pricing pages are prepared, but real payment processing still needs to be connected and tested.

Does cloud sync work?

Not yet. Cloud storage requires a backend, secure authentication, a database or file store, account management, and server-side storage limits. The website alone cannot provide those services.

What happens if my subscription ends?

The proposed policy is a three-day grace period, followed by suspension of subscription-only cloud access. A separate retention period and export warnings must be implemented and disclosed before launch. Local app use should remain available.

What if a payment fails?

The proposed policy allows seven days to recover a failed subscription payment before subscription-only cloud access is suspended. The final payment terms must explain what happens next.

Will cloud storage purchases be permanent?

The intended model is for storage add-ons to be one-time capacity purchases. They still require a working cloud service and clear terms before they can be sold.

Can I keep a local backup?

The app's actual export and backup features depend on the app build you publish. Test restoring a backup before promising that a particular backup format works.

Can prices or policies change?

They may change before launch. Publish final pricing, subscription renewal and cancellation rules, privacy details, and cloud retention terms before accepting payments.

How do I contact the creator?

A public contact email has not been configured on this template. Add an email address you control before publishing a support contact.

Keep it honest.

The website should describe features accurately, protect customer data, explain subscription terms clearly, and respect applicable consumer rights. A posted policy cannot override legal protections.
